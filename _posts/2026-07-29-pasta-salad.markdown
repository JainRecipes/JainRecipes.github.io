This refreshing pasta salad is the perfect make-ahead dish for picnics, potlucks, or easy weeknight meals. Tender pasta is tossed with crisp, colorful vegetables and a simple homemade dressing for a light yet satisfying combination of fresh flavors and textures. Serve it chilled as a side dish or enjoy it as a wholesome meal on its own.

#### Serves 8 people

#### Ingredients

|             Ingredients              |       Quantity        |
|:------------------------------------:|:---------------------:|
|            Fusilli pasta             |         1 lb.         |
|      Chickpeas (drained & rinsed)    |     1 (15-oz.) can    |
|      Cherry tomatoes (halved)        |        1 pint         |
|    Mini sweet peppers (thinly sliced)|         1 cup         |
|    Kalamata olives (pitted, halved)  |        3/4 cup        |
|              Cucumber                |           1           |
|               Carrot                 |           1           |
|           Salad dressing             |      As desired       |


## Directions

1. Cook pasta in a large pot of generously salted water until al dente. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside.
2. In a large bowl, combine drained pasta, chickpeas, tomatoes, bell peppers, etc. Pour dressing overtop and toss well to combine. Refrigerate for at least 1 to 2 hours before serving.

#### Notes

* Refrigerate leftover pasta salad for up to 3 days. When enjoying leftovers, add a drizzle of olive oil to help rehydrate the noodles. Leftovers can be enjoyed chilled or closer to room temperature.
